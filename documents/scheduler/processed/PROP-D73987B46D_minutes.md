To consider the request of the student for a make-up examination in course CS-302 due to a medical emergency, in accordance with Clause 6.2.4 of the Institute ordinances.

* The committee referenced past precedent (Case ID: DUGC/2024/CSE/014) regarding make-up examination protocols under Clause 6.2.4.

**3. Final Decisions:**
* Resolved that the application of the student for a make-up examination in CS-302 due to medical reasons, submitted via Form BP-10, be reviewed subject to human verification of the submission timeline and supporting medical documents as per Clause 6.2.4.
* Resolved further that upon obtaining the necessary course coordinator approval and HoD recommendation, the application be forwarded to the Chairperson, SUGC, for final permission, noting that the upper limit for awarding marks in the make-up test shall be 70% of the maximum marks (maximum 17.5 out of 25) in accordance with Clause 6.2.4.
