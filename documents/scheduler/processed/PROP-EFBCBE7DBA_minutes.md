**DRAFT MINUTES OF MEETING**
**Ref:** PROP-EFBCBE7DBA
**Date:** 2026-10-25

**1. Approved Agenda:**
To consider the request of the Ph.D. scholar for a time extension for thesis submission and review the submitted thesis evaluation reports in accordance with the applicable ordinance clauses.

**2. Notes & Member Observations:**
* The committee noted that while Form DP-17 (Examiners' Reports) has been included in the application, the mandatory IPR Cell similarity certificate (Form DP-14) confirming a similarity index of less than 15% as required under Section 13, Clause 13.1 has not been provided in the current records.
* The members observed that the explicit recommendation of the supervisor—which is a procedural prerequisite under Section 7, Clause 7.3 and Section 13, Clause 13.1 for granting an extension of the programme or thesis submission—is currently missing from the dossier.
* The committee referenced past precedent (Case ID: DDPC/2023/ME/033), reinforcing that compliance with plagiarism caps and procedural prerequisites must be strictly enforced prior to forwarding extension requests to higher authorities.

**3. Final Decisions:**
* Resolved that the request of the Ph.D. scholar who has completed 5 years in the programme for a time extension for thesis submission along with Form DP-17 be reviewed.
* Subject to human verification of the supervisor's recommendation and fulfillment of the IPR Cell similarity requirements via Form DP-14 as mandated under Clause 13.1, the DDPC may recommend the extension case to the Chairperson SDPC and Dean (Academic) pursuant to Clauses 7.3 and 13.1.
