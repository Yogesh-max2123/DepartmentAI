**DRAFT MINUTES OF MEETING**
**Ref:** PROP-4EA516F694
**Date:** 2026-10-23

**1. Approved Agenda:**
To review the 4-week unauthorized absence and academic probation status of a stipendiary M.Tech. student in accordance with relevant Institute ordinance clauses.

**2. Notes & Member Observations:**
* The committee noted that the student is currently on academic probation with a previous semester SPI of 4.8 and has been absent from campus for 4 weeks without any sanctioned leave or prior approval.
* It was observed that essential evidentiary documents, including official medical records, a formal explanation letter from the student regarding the unauthorized absence, and supervisor reports, are currently missing from the record.
* The members reviewed the applicability of Clause 5.1 and Clause 5.4, acknowledging that long absence without sanctioned leave violates the ordinance and warrants strict disciplinary and academic action.

**3. Final Decisions:**
* Resolved that the case of the stipendiary M.Tech. student currently on academic probation (SPI 4.8) who remained absent without sanctioned leave for 4 weeks be reviewed in accordance with Clause 5.4.
* Resolved further that the loss of financial assistantship for the 4-week period of unauthorized absence be implemented as per Clause 5.4, and the matter regarding the long unauthorized absence and academic probation status be placed before the DMPC and SMPC for further recommendations regarding potential programme termination to the Dean (Academic).
