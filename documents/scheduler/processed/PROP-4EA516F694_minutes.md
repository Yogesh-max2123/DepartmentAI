**DRAFT MINUTES OF MEETING**
**Ref:** PROP-4EA516F694
**Date:** 2026-10-24

**1. Approved Agenda:**
To review the second Comprehensive Examination of a stipendiary Ph.D. scholar after first-attempt failure, in accordance with Clause 9 of the Doctoral Ordinances.

**2. Notes & Member Observations:**
* The committee noted that the scholar failed the first Comprehensive Examination and that Clause 9 permits only one additional attempt.
* It was observed that Form DP-08 proposing the second board was on record, while a dated notice of the second examination had not yet been issued.
* The members reviewed Clauses 9 and 12.3 and noted that a second failure shall lead to termination by the Head, reported to the Senate.

**3. Final Decisions:**
* Resolved that a second Comprehensive Examination be conducted for the scholar as permitted by Clause 9, with the board constituted under Clause 12.3.
* Resolved further that the scholar be informed in writing that a second unsuccessful attempt shall result in termination of the Doctoral Programme.
