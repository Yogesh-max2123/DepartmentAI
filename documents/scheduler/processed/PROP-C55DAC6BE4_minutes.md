**DRAFT MINUTES OF MEETING**
**Ref:** PROP-C55DAC6BE4
**Date:** 2026-10-23

**1. Approved Agenda:**
To review and deliberate upon a final-semester M.Tech. student's request for an immediate change of thesis supervisor arising from a research publication authorship dispute.

**2. Notes & Member Observations:**
- The committee noted that under Ordinance Clause 11.1 (4), the change or addition of supervisor(s) is permitted through the DMPC, provided the decision is communicated to the Chairperson SMPC within one week along with the meeting minutes.
- Members observed critical deficiencies in the current application, specifically the absence of a formal written grievance concerning the authorship dispute, the lack of written consent or a statement from the current supervisor, and the absence of details or willingness from a proposed new supervisor to take over the thesis at this advanced stage.
- Precedent was acknowledged (referencing Case ID: DMPC/2023/CE/019), while noting that mediation or escalation to the Head of the Department may be necessary to resolve the underlying conflict prior to executing a late-stage supervisor change.

**3. Final Decisions:**
- Resolved that the application of the final semester M.Tech. student for an immediate change of thesis supervisor due to a research publication authorship dispute be reviewed by the DMPC subject to verification of supporting documentation.
- Resolved further that any approved change of supervisor under Clause 11.1 (4) be communicated to the Chairperson SMPC by the Convener DMPC within one week along with the meeting minutes, subject to human verification and final approval.
