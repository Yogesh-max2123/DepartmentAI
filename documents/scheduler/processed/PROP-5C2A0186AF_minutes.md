**DRAFT MINUTES OF MEETING**
**Ref:** PROP-5C2A0186AF
**Date:** 2026-10-25

**1. Approved Agenda:**
To review the academic standing and comprehensive examination performance of a full-time Ph.D. scholar who failed their first Comprehensive Examination and holds a Cumulative Performance Index (CPI) below the mandatory continuation threshold.

**2. Notes & Member Observations:**
* The committee noted that the full-time Ph.D. scholar failed their first Comprehensive Examination, and as per Clause 9, they are eligible for one final second attempt, failing which mandatory termination applies.
* The committee observed that the scholar's current CPI has dropped to 6.2, which falls below the minimum required continuation threshold of 6.5 stipulated under Clause 8.1(1)(a).
* The members noted that while Form DP-09 for the failed comprehensive examination is on record, specific course-wise grade sheets detailing the CPI drop are currently missing from the dossier and require subsequent administrative review.

**3. Final Decisions:**
* **Resolved that** the case of the full-time Ph.D. scholar who has failed their first Comprehensive Examination and whose CPI has dropped to 6.2 be placed before the DDPC for review.
* **Resolved further that**, as per Clause 9, the scholar be permitted to appear for a second Comprehensive Examination, subject to the explicit condition that a second failure shall result in mandatory termination from the Ph.D. programme as per institutional ordinances.
* **Resolved further that** in view of the scholar's current CPI of 6.2 falling below the minimum requirement of 6.5 under Clause 8.1(1)(a), the scholar be placed under academic monitoring to take necessary corrective measures to restore their CPI to the required minimum threshold.
