**DRAFT MINUTES OF MEETING**
**Ref:** PROP-0CD64DA7FE
**Date:** 2026-10-27

**1. Approved Agenda:** 
To review the academic standing and Comprehensive Examination status of a full-time Ph.D. scholar with a CPI below the minimum threshold and a failed first attempt at the examination.

**2. Notes & Member Observations:**
* The committee noted that the scholar's current CPI has dropped to 6.2, which violates the minimum required threshold of 6.5 stipulated under Section 8, Clause 8.1(1)(a).
* It was observed that the scholar has failed their first Comprehensive Examination, with Form DP-09 duly recorded, necessitating a review under Section 9, Clause 9.6.
* The members highlighted that while a second Comprehensive Examination is permitted under the ordinances, a subsequent failure will mandate termination from the programme, and noted the absence of specific departmental academic counseling logs or remediation measures.

**3. Final Decisions:**
* Resolved that the case of the full-time Ph.D. scholar who failed their first Comprehensive Examination (Form DP-09 on record) and holds a current CPI of 6.2 be reviewed by the DDPC.
* Subject to human verification, the committee recommends that the scholar be permitted a second and final attempt at the Comprehensive Examination pursuant to Clause 9.6.
* Recommended that academic measures be applied in accordance with Clause 8.1(1)(a) to address the CPI deficiency below 6.5.
